The bundled FFmpeg H.264 decoder and parser were built without --enable-gpl or

All bundled libraries are dynamically linked. In an installed session the
program is under $XDG_RUNTIME_DIR/quiet-rdp-app/0.3.1-<package-id>/; portable
archives use quiet-rdp-0.3.1-tails7-amd64/. With the app closed, a recipient may
replace compatible shared libraries in lib/ and relaunch run.sh. There is no
runtime signature restriction on replacement libraries. Modifying libraries
and reverse engineering to debug such modifications are permitted as required
by their licenses. The installer validates its original transport archive;
that check does not prevent replacement in the installed lib/ directory.
