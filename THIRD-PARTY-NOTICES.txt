Quiet RDP third-party notices

Quiet RDP uses FreeRDP 3.32.2 under Apache-2.0, FFmpeg decoder libraries under
LGPL-2.1-or-later, and other shared libraries under their respective licenses.
The application owner's binary license does not replace their terms. See
licenses/ and runtime-manifest.json for exact packages and notices. GTK 3,
GLib and the other libraries GTK links are not included: they are the copies
Tails itself ships.

The optional Wubi input method bundles unmodified Debian ibus-table programs
and ibus-table-wubi data. These run as separate IBus components and retain their
own licenses, including GPL terms identified in licenses/ibus-table.copyright
and licenses/ibus-table-wubi.copyright. Their corresponding source packages
are included in the same third-party source archive below.

The separate Rime input-method engine, its libraries and bundled input schemas
and dictionaries retain their respective Debian package licenses. Screenshot
recognition uses dynamically linked ZBar, Tesseract and Leptonica, with the
unmodified Debian English and Simplified Chinese recognition data. Exact
versions, file hashes and source packages are recorded in runtime-manifest.json;
their notices and corresponding source packages are also included.

The exact corresponding third-party sources and the build instructions are at:
https://github.com/tom062119/quiet-rdp-download/releases/tag/v0.6.0
Asset: quiet-rdp-0.6.0-third-party-sources.tar.gz
This archive contains third-party component sources, not Quiet RDP business code.

The bundled FFmpeg H.264 decoder and parser were built without --enable-gpl or
--enable-nonfree and without source modifications. The complete configure and
build commands are included as prepare-build.sh in the third-party archive.

All bundled libraries are dynamically linked. Portable archives extract to
quiet-rdp-0.6.0-tails7-amd64/. With the app closed, a recipient may replace
compatible shared libraries in lib/ and relaunch Quiet-RDP or run.sh. There is no
runtime signature restriction on replacement libraries. Modifying libraries
and reverse engineering to debug such modifications are permitted as required
by their licenses. Archive checksums do not prevent replacement in the
extracted lib/ directory.

The application and its portable launcher are not claimed to own third-party code.
Each library's copyright holder is identified by its included license notice.
